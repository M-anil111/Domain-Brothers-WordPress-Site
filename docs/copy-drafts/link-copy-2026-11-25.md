## 2. Domain buy service (`/buy-domains-service/`): decide first

Three pages now aim at the same search intent, "get a name someone else owns":

| Page | Focus keyword | What its text says |
|---|---|---|
| `/domain-brokerage-services/` | domain broker | a broker buys or sells for you, confidentially |
| `/acquire-premium-domain/` | premium domain acquisition | a broker approaches the owner; no fee unless we get it |
| `/buy-domains-service/` | domain buy service | its guide: "How a domain buy service gets you a name someone else owns" |

Google picks one page per intent and the others fade, so more links to the third page would add
to the competition rather than win anything.

**Recommended: merge.** Move the buy-service page's unique parts (consultation, research,
after-sale support, domain privacy) onto `/acquire-premium-domain/`, then 301-redirect
`/buy-domains-service/` there. The menu and existing links then point to one strong page.

**If you keep it,** give it a different job: help choosing and buying a *listed* name, with the
page rewritten to match. The links would then be:

- Guide "How to choose a domain name" (`/how-to-choose-a-domain-name/`), end of the shortlist
  section:
  > Want a second opinion before you buy? Our **domain buy service** helps you compare a shortlist,
  > check each name and complete the purchase.
- `/find-a-domain/`, under the results:
  > Torn between two names? The **domain buy service** helps you choose.
