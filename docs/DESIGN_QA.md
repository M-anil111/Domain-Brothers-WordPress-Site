## Notes for the developer and designer (checked by eye)

- **Buy Now (DB-12):** dark, and Buy Now and Make an Offer are the same width (256px = 256px at 320px). The corners are about 8px (a rounded rectangle), not fully round. Confirm whether "pill" means fully round; if it does, set `border-radius: 999px` on `.db-act-buy` and `.db-act-offer`.
- **Checkout container:** the script fell back to the page wrapper (`#page`, 0px). Check visually that the checkout columns follow the 1320px / 15px rule.
- **Section padding:** some failures are cards inside a page rather than page sections (`dbpp-calc`, `dbpp-cta`, `dblk-src`, `dbtp-rel`, `dbr-fd-cta`, the sign-in card `db-au`). Decide whether the 80 / 60 / 45px rule applies to them; the page-level misses are `dbl-head` (All Domains, Deals, Sold), the sold domain page bands, and every `dbx` section at 1024px (80px instead of 60px: the tablet breakpoint starts below 1024px).
- **Payment plan page H1:** the theme template's only H1 is a hidden contact line; the visible heading ("Choose your payment plan for ...") is an H2. Make the visible heading the H1.
- **False alarms already ruled out by hand:** the checkout total ($10,125.00 shows correctly), the filtered count ("Showing 1-72 of 72 matching your filters"), and the breadcrumbs (one line; items differ by 4px vertically).
