## Our rule
**All sales are final. Domain Brothers does not give refunds, returns, exchanges or credits once a payment has been made, for any reason.** This applies to every purchase made through DomainBrothers.com, whether you pay in full or on a payment plan, and to every payment, instalment, interest charge and fee.

## Why
A domain name is a unique digital asset. It is delivered electronically, it cannot be handed back in the state it was sold in, and once a transfer has started we cannot undo it. Sellers of domain names work this way across the industry. By paying, you accept that the sale cannot be reversed.

## What this covers
No refund, return, exchange or credit is available for any of these reasons, whether the reason is yours or not:
- You change your mind, no longer need the name, or cannot afford it.
- You find the same or a similar name elsewhere, or for less.
- You mistyped, misspelled or chose the wrong name or extension. Check the exact spelling and extension before you pay.
- A trademark owner or anyone else objects to the name, or you receive a complaint or a dispute notice. Checking trademarks and legal rights before you buy is your responsibility.
- The name does not bring the traffic, search ranking, customers, resale value or income you expected. We do not promise any of these.
- Your plans for the name, your business or your website change.
- Your registrar, hosting provider or bank delays or blocks the transfer, or you give us incorrect registrar details.
- You are charged renewal, registrar, bank, currency or other third-party fees.
- You do not use the name, or you let it expire after the transfer.

## Offers and agreed prices
An offer we have accepted in writing is a commitment to buy at that price. An accepted offer is a sale and is final when you pay.

## Payment plans
Every instalment you pay is non-refundable, and so is any interest in the plan. Stopping or cancelling future payments does not entitle you to a refund of what you have paid. If a payment is not made, the plan ends, the domain returns to our inventory and may be sold to someone else, and the payments already made are kept and are not credited toward any other purchase. The full rules are in our [Payment Terms](/payment-terms/).

## Chargebacks and payment disputes
If you think a charge is wrong, contact us first at [sales@domainbrothers.com](mailto:sales@domainbrothers.com). We will look into it quickly.

If you start a chargeback or payment dispute for a purchase you made and received, we will contest it with our records, including your order, your recorded acceptance of our terms and the transfer history. While a dispute is open we may suspend or cancel the domain and related services, and we may recover dispute fees and costs to the extent the law allows. We may refuse future sales to anyone who disputes a valid purchase.

## Two things that are not refunds of a sale
These are corrections, not returns of a completed sale:
- **A charge we make in error.** If we take a payment by mistake, for example the same instalment twice, we reverse the extra charge.
- **An order we cannot deliver.** If we decline an order, or cannot deliver the domain before it has been transferred to you, the payment for that order is returned to the card it came from.

## Your legal rights
This policy applies as far as the law allows. If the law where you live gives you a right that cannot be waived, this policy does not take that right away.

## Before you buy
Take a few minutes to check that:
- the name and extension are exactly what you want;
- you have searched the trademark register in each country where you will use the name;
- the price, and the payment plan if you choose one, fit your budget, including renewals at your registrar;
- you have read our [Terms of Use](/terms-of-use/) and [Payment Terms](/payment-terms/).

## Questions
Write to [sales@domainbrothers.com](mailto:sales@domainbrothers.com) before you pay if anything is unclear. Asking a question does not oblige us to issue a refund.

By paying on DomainBrothers.com you confirm that you have read, understood and agree to this Refund Policy.
