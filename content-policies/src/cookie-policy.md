## 1. What cookies are
Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in the same way. We use them to keep the site working, to remember choices, and to understand how the site is used.

## 2. What we use
| Type | What it does | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | Keeps you signed in, protects forms from bots and keeps the site secure. The site does not work properly without them. | Sign-in session (db_wl, up to 30 days), WordPress session and security cookies, Cloudflare bot-check cookies |
| Preferences | Remembers choices you make. | Your Grid or List view on domain lists (stored in your browser) |
| Measurement | Counts visits and shows how pages are used, so we can improve the site. | Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity |
| Marketing measurement | Measures how people reach the site and how campaigns perform. | Google Ads measurement, HubSpot |

We do not currently show advertising on the site. If we turn on advertising, we will update this policy first.

## 3. Third parties
Some cookies are set by the providers named above. They have their own privacy policies, and we do not control their cookies. Cloudflare delivers some of these tools for us.

## 4. Your choices
- Your browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking essential cookies will stop sign-in and some forms from working.
- You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on, and you can use your browser's "Do Not Track" or privacy settings.
- Marketing emails are separate from cookies. Unsubscribe at any time with the link in each email, or in your account Profile.

## 5. Changes
We may update this policy when we change the tools we use. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
