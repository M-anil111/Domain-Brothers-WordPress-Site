1. Why this policy exists

It keeps the site safe and useful for buyers and keeps domain names from being used to harm people. It applies to everyone who uses the site, our forms, our accounts, our email and any domain that is held or transferred through us. It forms part of our Terms of Use.

2. On the site

You must not:

  • break the law, or help anyone else to;
  • give false or misleading information, or pretend to be someone else;
  • make an offer or place an order you do not intend to complete;
  • use another person's card or payment details without permission, or dispute a valid payment dishonestly;
  • try to get around our security, bot checks, rate limits or account controls;
  • scrape, harvest or copy the site, its listings or its prices by automated means, or overload it;
  • upload or send malware, or interfere with how the site works;
  • attempt to buy or hold names to resell to a trademark owner for a profit, or to cause confusion with a brand.

3. Spam and unsolicited offers

Our contact forms, enquiry form, account and email are for people who want to buy, sell or ask about domains and our services. Do not use them to send unsolicited commercial messages. This includes offers of web development, design, app development, marketing, search engine optimisation, link building, guest posts or sponsored content, lead lists, "services" for our website, and pharmacy, gambling, adult or get-rich-quick material.

We use hidden fields, timing checks, content filters and bot protection to block these messages. We may report them, block the sender, and keep a record of the attempt.

4. With a domain you buy from us

During a payment plan the domain stays registered in our custody. During the plan and after the transfer, you must not use the name:

  • for phishing, fraud, malware, spam or botnets;
  • for content that is illegal where it is hosted or where it is viewed;
  • to infringe a trademark or copyright, or to imitate another business, person or website;
  • for anything that breaks the rules of the registry or registrar.

5. What we may do

If we think this policy has been broken, we may remove a listing, decline or cancel an order, suspend or end a payment plan, suspend an account, hold or take back a domain that is still in our custody, refuse future sales, and report the matter to payment providers, registrars, registries and the authorities. Payments already made are not refunded, as the Refund Policy explains.

6. Reporting a problem

To report abuse, a suspicious message or a listing that infringes rights, write to sales@domainbrothers.com. For trademark and copyright complaints, see our Trademark and Copyright Notices.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
